# Faction, Commander, and Character Bible

## Faction Set

Long-term faction identities:

- Solari: gold, plasma, stars, radiant shields
- Voidborn: purple/black, distortion, biomass, gravity hunger
- Synthari: cyan, grids, circuits, drones, machine logic
- Verdant: green, bioluminescent, spores, living armor
- Crimson: red, volcanic, blood-metal, aggression
- Astral: blue/white, portals, constellations, time

Current prototype factions map like this:

- Solari stays Solari.
- Voidborn stays Voidborn.
- Quantum becomes Synthari, or is folded into Synthari as its high-tech branch.
- Crystalline becomes Astral/mineral ancient tech, or a neutral defensive card family.
- Reavers become neutral raiders, campaign enemies, or a later playable expansion faction.

## Commander Rules

Commanders are the player's face in battle.

Each commander has:

- HP
- Passive ability
- Active ability
- Ascended ability
- Faction visual identity
- Campaign progression track
- Cosmetic evolution stages

Alpha command zones:

- Enemy commander floats above the top row.
- Player commander anchors the bottom row.
- Commanders are not grid pieces in alpha.
- Some abilities target board sectors.

## Solari Concord

Visual language:

- Gold plasma
- Radiant shields
- Star halos
- White-hot blades
- Smooth temple geometry

Playstyle:

- Control
- Healing
- Shields
- Influence
- Defensive structures

Weakness:

- Slower pressure
- Vulnerable to armor break, corruption, and wide boards

Core mechanics:

- Shield
- Radiant
- Bastion
- Purify
- Treaty
- Rebirth

Commander:

Name:

- Vael'Sun, Dawn Herald

Role:

- Battle commander of the Concord's first expedition through the shattered Veil.

Passive:

- First Light: the first ally you deploy each turn gains Shield 1.

Active:

- Radiant Verdict, 2 Resonance: deal 1 damage to an enemy. If it is on a corrupted or enemy world, deal 2 instead.

Ascended:

- Solar Rebirth: once per match, the first allied entity that dies returns to your deploy row with 1 HP and Shield 2.

Character notes:

- Vael'Sun believes peace is possible only after the battlefield is made orderly.
- Voice tone should be calm, absolute, and severe.

## Voidborn Swarm

Visual language:

- Purple-black biomass
- Tendrils
- Distortion
- Teeth, sacs, and gravity wells
- Sickly violet glow

Playstyle:

- Swarm
- Sacrifice
- Corruption
- Consume
- Board flooding

Weakness:

- Fragile pieces
- Can run out of board space
- Weak to purge and area damage

Core mechanics:

- Spawn
- Consume
- Corrupt
- Brood
- Assimilate
- Hunger

Commander:

Name:

- Vzaal, Apex Brood

Role:

- A concentrated mouth of the Hive Mind, formed when the swarm needs strategy instead of appetite.

Passive:

- Endless Brood: the first time a friendly organism dies each turn, spawn a 1/1 Broodling in an adjacent empty sector.

Active:

- Assimilate, 2 Resonance: destroy a friendly entity. Give another friendly entity +1/+1 and heal your commander for 1.

Ascended:

- World Hunger: when you control three Organic or Corrupted worlds, your Broodlings gain +1 attack.

Character notes:

- Vzaal should feel plural: "We tasted your future."
- The commander portrait can subtly multiply or split.

- Hard-light panels

- Circuit constellations

Playstyle:

- Attachments
- Drones
- Automation

- Cost reduction through infrastructure

Weakness:

- Needs setup
- Structures can be raided
- Low raw stats before upgrades attach

Core mechanics:

- Drone
- Attach
- Reconfigure
- Relay
- Overclock
- Scan

Commander:

Name:

- Unit-0 Lyra, Prime Architect

Role:

- The first Synthari mind to design itself after the Convergence.

Passive:

- Modular Warform: the first attachment you play each turn costs 1 less.

Active:

- Fabricate Drone, 2 Resonance: create a 1/1 Drone on an adjacent controlled sector.

Ascended:

- Perfect Network: if you control three connected Machine sectors, your drones gain +1/+1 and can move immediately.

Character notes:

- Lyra should sound precise, curious, and unnervingly calm.
- Commander animation should show scanning lines and modular panels.

Commander:

Name:

- Myca Vey, Spore Oracle

Role:

Commander:

Name:

- Korr Vane, Crucible Warlord

Role:

- A warlord who believes civilizations are only proven real when they survive the forge.

Passive:

- Blood-Metal Oath: the first friendly entity damaged each turn gains +1 attack.

Active:

- Crucible Strike, 2 Resonance: deal 1 damage to a friendly entity and 2 damage to an adjacent enemy.

Ascended:

- Redline War: when your commander is at 15 HP or less, all friendly damaged entities gain Frenzy.

Character notes:

- Korr should feel loud, direct, and physical.
- Animation should include sparks, heat shimmer, and armor plates.

- Stasis

Commander:

Name:

- Ilyon, Star-Warden

Role:

- Keeper of the old gates between dead galaxies.

Passive:

- Seen Before: once per turn, after you draw, Forecast 1. You may leave the card or send it to the bottom.

Character notes:

- Ilyon should feel ancient, precise, and sad.

## Neutral Families

Neutral cards should support all factions without stealing identity.

Neutral families:

- Barren settlers: cheap worlds and basic harvesters
- Crystalline ancients: defense, stasis, mineral worlds
- Nebula Reavers: speed, salvage, disruption
- Guild artificers: generic attachments
- Nomad crews: domain workers and campaign support
- Anomaly beasts: campaign enemies and wild cards

## Keyword Starter List

Shield:

- Prevent the next X damage.

Guardian:

- Adjacent or protected allies cannot be targeted by melee attacks while this can be attacked.

Strike First:

- Can attack the turn it is deployed.

Frenzy:

- After this survives damage, gain +1 attack until end of turn or permanently depending on card text.

Regenerate:

- Heal at start of turn.

Spore Mark:

- Takes 1 damage at end step and counts as infected.

Corrupt:

- Change a sector to Corrupted. Voidborn and some enemies benefit from it.

Blink:

- Move without crossing intervening sectors.

Forecast:

- Look at upcoming card(s) and optionally reorder or bottom.

Stasis:

- Cannot move, attack, or be damaged until stasis ends, depending on effect.

Overclock:

- Temporary power with a delayed cost.

Pierce:

- Ignores Shield or Guardian depending on card text.

Overwhelm:

- Excess damage spills to another valid target.

Deploy Beacon:

- Allows deployment adjacent to this object.

## Character Progression

Commanders should have progression that feels visual, not just numeric.

Progression layers:

- Level: unlocks cards and cosmetics
- Bond: unlocks dialogue, faction lore, alternate animations
- Ascension: unlocks alternate passive/active choices
- Relics: campaign-only modifiers
- Skins: visual identity, no power in PvP

PvP fairness rule:

- Competitive modes should normalize power.
- Campaign can allow wild permanent upgrades.
