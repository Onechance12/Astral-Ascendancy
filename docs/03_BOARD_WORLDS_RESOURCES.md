# Worlds, Structures, Resources, and Domain

## Core Idea

Worlds are the bridge between battle, collection, campaign, and domain.

A world can be:

- A card in a deck
- A sector on the 5x5 battle board
- A campaign planet
- A permanent domain location
- A resource generator
- A faction identity piece

This is where Astral Ascendancy can become more than another card battler.

## World Card Rules

World cards are played onto sectors.

Default rules:

- Must target an empty neutral sector or an empty sector you control.
- Cannot be played under an enemy entity unless a card says so.
- Can upgrade compatible worlds you control.
- Can be destroyed, corrupted, sealed, or ruined by some effects.
- Structures usually require a world underneath them.

World cards should visually change the board sector immediately.

## Resource System

### Match Resources

Resonance:

- Universal turn-by-turn energy.
- Drives card play and active abilities.

Influence:

- Board-control victory resource.
- Generated by controlling sectors, worlds, and spires.

Temporary Materials:

- Some worlds or structures create temporary Plasma, Biomass, Alloy, Ember, Verdance, or Aether during a match.
- These should be limited in alpha so the UI stays readable.

### Account/Domain Resources

Aether Shards:

- Crafting and duplicate conversion currency.

Plasma:

- Star-world production.
- Used for Solari upgrades, shields, reactors, radiant cosmetics.

Biomass:

- Organic-world production.
- Used for Voidborn/Verdant upgrades, creature evolution, growth systems.

Alloy:

- Machine/mineral/crucible production.
- Used for Synthari/Crimson structures, armor, weapons, domain buildings.

Ember:

- Crucible-world production.
- Used for Crimson upgrades, aggressive weapons, forge events.

Verdance:

- Verdant-world production.
- Used for living armor, spores, healing facilities.

Aether:

- Astral-world production.
- Used for portals, stasis, campaign travel, rare crafting.

Data:

- Machine-world production.
- Used for Synthari automation, scanning, AI assistants, research.

Crystal:

- Mineral-world production.
- Used for defensive upgrades, refraction, premium-looking but earnable cosmetics.

Tritium:

- Gas-world production.
- Used for ships, operations, campaign travel, future raids.

## Science and Engineering

Worlds should advance through research, not only produce resources.

Science turns repeated play into civilization progress:

- Star worlds support Stellar Physics, shield labs, and purification research.
- Organic, Corrupted, and Verdant worlds support Xenobiology, spores, regeneration, and organism evolution.
- Machine worlds support Synthetic Systems, drones, scans, and automation.
- Crucible worlds support War Metallurgy, stress testing, siege engines, and forge upgrades.
- Astral worlds support Temporal Mechanics, Forecast, Blink, Stasis, and portal calibration.
- Barren, Mineral, and Gas worlds support Planetary Engineering, logistics, surveying, and resource conversion.

Engineering projects are buildable upgrades that consume resources and require science progress.

Examples:

- Solar Reactor Array: improves Star structure Plasma output.
- Brood Incubator Array: improves organism spawn infrastructure.
- Drone Fabricator: unlocks Machine-world drone production.
- Worldroot Irrigation: improves Verdant healing and spread.
- Siege Engine Works: improves Crimson structure damage.
- Portal Calibration Rig: improves Astral movement safety.

For the full system, see `docs/14_SCIENCE_ENGINEERING_PROGRESSION.md`.

## Structures

Structures should feel powerful only if protected.

Structure rules:

- Played onto controlled world sectors.
- Usually cannot move.
- Usually cannot attack immediately.
- Trigger at start of turn or end of turn.
- Give clear radius indicators.
- Cost tempo because they occupy a sector and do not immediately fight.

## Structure Catalog

### Solar Reactor

World requirement:

- Star World

Effect:

- Start of turn: gain 1 temporary Resonance.
- If protected for three turns, gain 2 Influence.

Visual:

- Vertical gold beam and rotating shield rings.

### Dawn Spire

World requirement:

- Star World

Effect:

- Adjacent allies gain Shield 1 at start of turn.
- If three allies are shielded, gain 1 Influence.

Visual:

- Tall radiant obelisk.

### Hive Nest

World requirement:

- Organic World

Effect:

- Start of turn: spawn a 1/1 Broodling if adjacent sector is empty.

Visual:

- Pulsing egg cluster.

### Biomass Vat

World requirement:

- Organic World

Effect:

- When a nearby entity dies, store 1 Biomass.
- Spend 2 stored Biomass to evolve a friendly organism.

Visual:

- Breathing sacs and fluid tubes.

### Relay Grid

World requirement:

- Machine World

Effect:

- Adjacent Synthari cards cost 1 less to activate.
- Connected Relay Grids extend deployment range.

Visual:

- Cyan hard-light lines between sectors.

### Drone Foundry

World requirement:

- Machine World

Effect:

- Start of turn: create a 1/1 Drone in hand or on an adjacent sector.

Visual:

- Holographic assembly arms.

### Worldroot Nursery

World requirement:

- Organic or Verdant World

Effect:

- Adjacent allies regenerate 1 HP.
- If it survives three turns, spread Verdant terrain to one adjacent empty sector.

Visual:

- Roots, spores, and glowing leaves.

### Spore Beacon

World requirement:

- Verdant World

Effect:

- Enemies entering adjacent sectors get Spore Mark.
- Spore Mark causes 1 damage at end step and can trigger Verdant effects.

Visual:

- Floating green spores.

### Crucible Forge

World requirement:

- Crucible World

Effect:

- Start of turn: create a temporary Weapon attachment.
- Friendly Crimson entities on adjacent sectors gain +1 attack when damaged.

Visual:

- Molten metal hammer and vents.

### Ember Cannon

World requirement:

- Crucible World

Effect:

- End of turn: deal 1 damage to the nearest enemy in same row or column.

Visual:

- Red artillery aperture.

### Astral Gate

World requirement:

- Astral World

Effect:

- Friendly entities can move between connected gates.
- If two gates are active for two turns, gain 3 Influence.

Visual:

- Blue-white portal ring.

### Time Observatory

World requirement:

- Astral World

Effect:

- Forecast the top card of your deck.
- Once per turn, you may put that card on the bottom.

Visual:

- Floating rings and star map.

### Crystal Bastion

World requirement:

- Mineral World

Effect:

- Adjacent sectors are Fortified.
- Fortified allies take 1 less damage.

Visual:

- Crystal wall plates.

### Refinery

World requirement:

- Gas, Mineral, Machine, or Barren World

Effect:

- Produces the world's base material for domain after match if it survives.

Visual:

- Industrial harvester with faction skin.

## Domain Loop

The domain is the player's living alien base.

Do not present it as a table. Present it as:

- A world cluster
- Orbiting planets
- Buildings and resource collectors
- Crew/commanders assigned to places
- Glowing reactors
- Harvest animations
- Alert signals
- Ships traveling between nodes

Domain flow:

1. Win campaign or battle rewards.
2. Acquire a world card, structure blueprint, crew, material, or card.
3. Add it to the domain.
4. Assign structures/crew to produce resources.
5. Use resources to craft cards, upgrade commanders, unlock cosmetics, or improve campaign capabilities.
6. Bring selected world cards back into future battles.

## Battle-to-Domain Conversion

Not every world played in battle becomes permanent.

Possible reward rules:

- Campaign planet cleared: unlock that planet as a domain world.
- Rare world card survives until victory: chance to duplicate as domain asset.
- Boss defeated on a world: claim boss world.
- Structure survives three battles: unlock upgraded blueprint.

## Alpha Resource Scope

For the first playable 5x5 prototype, keep it lean:

- Resonance
- Influence
- One temporary material per faction, shown only when relevant
- Aether Shards as account/crafting currency

Do not build the entire economy before the board is fun.
