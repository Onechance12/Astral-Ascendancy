# Rarity, Packs, Crafting, and Combine System

## Goal

Astral Ascendancy needs cards that feel collectible, rare, and exciting without turning into a pay-to-win economy.

The system should support:

- Common cards that teach mechanics.
- Rare cards that create build paths.
- Very rare cards that feel legendary.
- Duplicate protection.
- Crafting.
- Combining duplicates.
- Cosmetic flex.
- Campaign progression.
- Future paid packs without destroying trust.

## Core Rule

Rarity controls excitement, complexity, animation treatment, and collection progression.

Rarity should not mean:

- Paid-only power.
- Impossible-to-craft meta staples.
- Duplicate upgrades that overpower PvP.
- Hidden odds.

## Rarity Ladder

### Common

Purpose:

- Teach mechanics.
- Fill starter decks.
- Provide reliable role players.

Visual:

- Silver frame.
- Small idle glow.

Gameplay:

- Simple text.
- One mechanic at most.
- Low combo dependency.

### Uncommon

Purpose:

- Introduce synergies.
- Reward positioning and faction play.

Visual:

- Green-blue accent line.
- Slight animated frame pulse.

Gameplay:

- One clear synergy hook.
- Often interacts with worlds, structures, or status effects.

### Rare

Purpose:

- Build-around cards.
- Strong worlds and structures.
- Cards that make a deck feel different.

Visual:

- Blue frame energy.
- Card hover has stronger parallax.

Gameplay:

- Enables a strategy.
- Still readable in one sentence.

### Holo

Purpose:

- Flashy faction identity cards.
- Strong but not always universal.

Visual:

- Animated holographic frame.
- Faction-specific shimmer.
- Played-card battlefield aura.

Gameplay:

- Major tactical swing or signature board object.
- Usually requires positioning or setup.

### Mythic

Purpose:

- Commander-level fantasy.
- Big pack moment.
- Faction-defining payoff.

Visual:

- Full animated frame.
- Entrance flourish.
- Unique sound hook.
- Strong on-board idle animation.

Gameplay:

- Powerful, but often conditional.
- Not required to play the faction starter strategy.

### Singularity

Purpose:

- Rare af cosmic cards.
- Expansion-level chase cards.
- Strange cards that bend rules.

Visual:

- Black-white gravitational lens.
- Background distortion.
- Slow reveal pause.
- Unique pack-opening ritual beat.

Gameplay:

- Rule-bending.
- Low count in alpha.
- Must be craftable or earnable through long-term play.
- Avoid being mandatory for core decks.

## Pack Odds: Design Target

These are design targets, not final legal/economy copy.

Default 5-card pack:

- Slot 1: at least Uncommon
- Slot 2: any rarity
- Slot 3: any rarity
- Slot 4: any rarity
- Slot 5: Rare-or-better slot

Base odds per non-guaranteed slot:

- Common: 68.0%
- Uncommon: 23.0%
- Rare: 7.0%
- Holo: 1.6%
- Mythic: 0.35%
- Singularity: 0.05%

Rare-or-better slot:

- Rare: 84.0%
- Holo: 12.0%
- Mythic: 3.5%
- Singularity: 0.5%

Pity:

- Holo guaranteed within 20 packs.
- Mythic guaranteed within 60 packs.
- Singularity progress visible, not hidden.
- Opening a Singularity resets Singularity pity only.

## Duplicate Protection

Rules:

- Never give a fourth playable copy of a card if another same-rarity card is missing, once deck copy limits are known.
- Extra duplicates convert to Aether Shards and card Memory.
- Mythic and Singularity duplicates should feel good, not insulting.

Duplicate conversion:

- Common: 5 shards, 1 Memory
- Uncommon: 15 shards, 2 Memory
- Rare: 50 shards, 5 Memory
- Holo: 150 shards, 12 Memory
- Mythic: 500 shards, 30 Memory
- Singularity: 2000 shards, 100 Memory plus a cosmetic token

## Combine System

Combining duplicates should make cards feel alive without breaking fair PvP.

Each card has a Memory track.

Memory sources:

- Duplicate pulls
- Campaign rewards
- Commander bond rewards
- Crafting sacrifice
- Event rewards later

Combine tiers:

### Tier 0: Base

- Playable card.
- Normal frame.

### Tier 1: Awakened

Requirement:

- Small Memory cost.

Unlocks:

- Animated card idle.
- Slight battlefield aura.
- Lore fragment.

Gameplay:

- No PvP stat change.

### Tier 2: Attuned

Requirement:

- Higher Memory cost.

Unlocks:

- Alternate VFX.
- Voice/sound hook.
- Campaign-only minor modifier slot.

Gameplay:

- PvP unchanged.
- Campaign modifier must be explicitly marked campaign-only.

### Tier 3: Ascended

Requirement:

- Major Memory cost plus faction material.

Unlocks:

- Full animated frame.
- Alternate art.
- Card title treatment.
- Optional sidegrade form if design supports it.

Gameplay:

- If it changes gameplay, it becomes a separate craftable card or loadout sidegrade.
- It must not be a raw duplicate-power upgrade in fair PvP.

### Tier 4: Celestial

Requirement:

- Long-term chase.

Unlocks:

- Cosmetic battlefield entrance.
- Premium pack reveal recall.
- Profile/banner flex.
- Domain statue or living display object.

Gameplay:

- No PvP stat change.

## Card Fusion

Fusion is different from duplicate combining.

Combining duplicates improves a card's presentation and progression.

Fusion creates a new card from ingredients.

Example:

- 2 Solari Acolytes + Radiant Aegis Memory + Plasma = Dawn Acolyte card variant.
- 3 Void Broodlings + Biomass + Void Nest Memory = Brood Hunter card variant.
- Gate Wisp + Phase Mantle Memory + Aether = Gate Wisp Ascendant sidegrade.

Fusion rules:

- Fused gameplay cards must be craftable directly.
- Fusion cannot be the only path to a competitive card.
- Fusions are good for campaign, collection goals, and thematic deck identity.

## Crafting

Crafting uses Aether Shards and sometimes faction materials.

Crafting should be slower for rare cards but always possible.

Suggested shard craft costs:

- Common: 40
- Uncommon: 120
- Rare: 400
- Holo: 1200
- Mythic: 4000
- Singularity: 16000

Faction material surcharge:

- Rare and above can require faction material.
- The material should be earnable through domain/campaign.

## Dusting/Sacrificing Cards

Avoid encouraging players to destroy their collection early.

If sacrificing exists:

- Only extra duplicates can be sacrificed by default.
- Full manual sacrifice unlocks later with warnings.
- Starter deck cards cannot be accidentally sacrificed.

## Deck Copy Limits

Alpha recommendation:

- Common: up to 3 copies
- Uncommon: up to 3 copies
- Rare: up to 2 copies
- Holo: up to 2 copies
- Mythic: up to 1 copy
- Singularity: up to 1 copy

Reason:

- Keeps rare cards special.
- Prevents Mythic/Singularity pile decks.
- Makes Commons/Uncommons strategically important.

## Rarity and Gameplay Complexity

Rarity should usually increase complexity, not raw efficiency.

Common:

- One job.

Uncommon:

- One job plus synergy.

Rare:

- Strategy anchor.

Holo:

- Major tactical moment.

Mythic:

- Signature payoff.

Singularity:

- Rule bend with clear counterplay.

## Rarity and Animation

Every rarity needs a pack and board treatment.

Common:

- Clean flip.
- Small sparkle.

Uncommon:

- Color sweep.
- Slight card hum.

Rare:

- Card pause.
- Blue flare.
- Faction sigil appears.

Holo:

- Rotating holographic refraction.
- Stronger sound.

Mythic:

- Screen darkens.
- Commander/faction callout.
- Card reveals with a burst.

Singularity:

- Sound drops out.
- Pack fragments orbit inward.
- Card bends background like gravity.
- Longest reveal.

## Fair Free-to-Play Economy

The game can sell packs later, but the foundation must feel fair.

Rules:

- Earn packs through play.
- Show odds.
- Show pity progress.
- Let players craft specific cards.
- Never require paid-only cards.
- Keep starter decks upgradeable through campaign.
- Keep cosmetic combine rewards valuable.

## Alpha Scope

For first playable alpha, implement only in design:

- Rarity assignments
- Pack reveal treatments
- Shard conversion rules
- Memory concept
- Combine tiers

Do not implement monetization.

When code begins, the first actual system should be:

- Rarity display
- Pack opening animation hooks
- Duplicate-to-shards placeholder
- Collection card detail showing Memory and combine tiers
