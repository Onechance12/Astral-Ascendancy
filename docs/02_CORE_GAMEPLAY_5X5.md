# Core Gameplay: 5x5 Living Board

## Goal

Replace the current 3-lane prototype direction with a 5x5 tactical board that stays simple to learn but has enough room for world cards, structures, movement, evolution, and faction identity.

## Match Fantasy

Two commanders arrive above an unstable alien world. Their cards are not just units. They are creatures, machines, structures, relics, weather systems, planetside colonies, and cosmic anomalies.

By the end of a match, the 5x5 board should look changed:

- Sectors claimed
- Worlds awakened
- Structures built
- Portals opened
- Corruption spread
- Shields cracked
- Ruins left behind
- Survivors evolved

## Board Layout

The board has 25 sectors:

```txt
Enemy Command Zone

E1 E2 E3 E4 E5   Enemy deploy row
F1 F2 F3 F4 F5   Enemy forward row
C1 C2 C3 C4 C5   Contested anomaly row
P1 P2 P3 P4 P5   Player forward row
D1 D2 D3 D4 D5   Player deploy row

Player Command Zone
```

Rows:

- Enemy Deploy Row: enemy can deploy most entities here.
- Enemy Forward Row: enemy pressure and structures.
- Contested Anomaly Row: center objective, world pressure, portals, resource nodes.
- Player Forward Row: player pressure and structures.
- Player Deploy Row: player can deploy most entities here.

The center sector, C3, is usually the Anomaly Core. It can begin neutral, revealed, hazardous, or scenario-specific.

## Match Length

Target:

- 6 to 10 turns for quick battles
- 10 to 14 turns for campaign/boss battles
- 8 to 12 minutes for normal PvE
- 6 to 10 minutes for future PvP

## Player Setup

Alpha baseline:

- Commander HP: 30
- Deck size: 24 for alpha, 30 long-term
- Starting hand: 4
- Mulligan: replace any number once
- Draw: 1 card per turn
- Max hand: 8
- Resonance starts at 1 and increases by 1 per turn to 10
- Unspent Resonance does not carry over by default

Long-term variations:

- Some worlds temporarily store Resonance.
- Some structures bank faction material.
- Some commanders alter starting hand, deck size, or max Resonance.

## Core Resources

### Resonance

The match energy resource. Used to play cards and activate many abilities.

- Starts at 1
- Increases each turn
- Caps at 10
- Spent on entities, anomalies, worlds, structures, attachments, and commander abilities

### Influence

The board-control victory and domain pressure resource.

Gained by:

- Holding center sectors
- Building banners/spires
- Completing world objectives
- Commander/faction effects

Influence win target:

- Alpha: 30 Influence
- Boss/campaign: scenario-specific

### Materials

Faction materials generated by worlds and structures. They matter more in domain/campaign, but some can appear mid-match.

- Plasma: Solari, star worlds, shields, energy weapons
- Biomass: Voidborn/Verdant, organic worlds, growth, spawn, healing
- Alloy: Synthari/Crimson, machines, armor, weapons, structures
- Ember: Crimson, volcano worlds, attack boosts, burn effects
- Verdance: Verdant, spores, regeneration, living armor
- Aether: Astral, portals, time, teleport, forecast

### Aether Shards

Account-level crafting/duplicate currency. Not the same as in-match Aether.

Used for:

- Crafting cards
- Unlocking cosmetics
- Upgrading domain facilities
- Future pack pity/crafting systems

## Turn Structure

Each turn follows a predictable rhythm:

1. Start of Turn
   - World effects pulse.
   - Structure effects trigger.
   - Damage-over-time and regeneration resolve.
   - Center anomaly updates.

2. Resource Step
   - Increase max Resonance if below cap.
   - Refill Resonance.
   - Add any temporary resource bonuses.

3. Draw Step
   - Draw one card.
   - Trigger draw effects.

4. Main Action Phase
   - Deploy entities.
   - Play world cards.
   - Build structures.
   - Play anomalies.
   - Attach armor, weapons, skills, or relics.
   - Move eligible entities.
   - Activate commander ability.

5. Combat Phase
   - Player chooses attacks with ready entities.
   - Some campaign enemies may show planned attacks before this phase.
   - Melee attacks can cause retaliation.
   - Ranged attacks do not cause retaliation unless target has Counterfire.

6. End Step
   - Check deaths.
   - Check sector control.
   - Award Influence.
   - Resolve evolution checks.
   - Check victory conditions.

## Actions

### Deploy Entity

Play an entity from hand onto a legal sector.

Default legal sectors:

- Own deploy row
- Controlled friendly forward row sectors
- Adjacent to a friendly structure with Deploy Beacon
- Adjacent to commander-linked portal

Entities enter exhausted unless they have Strike First, Ambush, or a faction ability.

### Move

Most entities may move once per turn.

Default movement:

- 1 orthogonal sector
- Cannot move through occupied sectors
- Cannot enter enemy-controlled hazardous sectors unless allowed

Movement traits:

- Scout: move 2, orthogonal path
- Flying: ignore terrain, cannot pass through occupied entities
- Burrow: pass under enemy zones, cannot enter mechanical sectors
- Blink: teleport to a visible legal sector
- Titan: cannot move unless displaced by special ability

### Attack

Most entities attack adjacent orthogonal enemies.

Attack ranges:

- Melee: adjacent orthogonal
- Reach: adjacent orthogonal or diagonal
- Ranged 2: up to two sectors in straight line
- Artillery: any revealed sector in same column or row, usually structure-only
- Beam: straight line, can hit multiple targets

Retaliation:

- Melee target retaliates if alive and able.
- Ranged target only retaliates if it has Counterfire or Ranged.
- Structures do not retaliate unless armed.

### Play World

World cards terraform sectors.

Default legal sectors:

- Empty neutral sector
- Empty sector you control
- Existing compatible world you control, as an upgrade

Worlds can:

- Change terrain
- Generate resources
- Add hazards
- Modify movement
- Enable structures
- Boost faction cards
- Create alternate victory conditions

### Build Structure

Structures are played onto controlled world sectors.

Structures:

- Usually cannot move
- Usually cannot attack immediately
- Have HP
- Produce value over time
- Are vulnerable to raids
- Often define the player's board economy

### Attach

Attachments modify an entity, commander, structure, or world.

Attachment types:

- Armor
- Weapon
- Skill
- Relic
- Parasite
- Drone
- Spore
- Sigil

Default limit:

- 1 Armor
- 1 Weapon
- 1 Skill
- 1 Relic

Some factions break these limits.

### Evolve

Entities can evolve when they meet conditions.

Common triggers:

- Survive a full turn
- Deal commander damage
- Kill an enemy
- Stand on matching world
- Receive enough attachments
- Control enough sectors
- Be consumed/reborn

Evolution should be visible:

- Card frame changes
- Model/sprite changes
- Name changes
- Audio cue
- Faction burst
- New ability icon

## Sector Control

Sectors can be:

- Neutral
- Player controlled
- Enemy controlled
- Contested
- Corrupted
- Fortified
- Sealed
- Ruined

Default control rules:

- A sector with your entity and no enemy influence becomes controlled by you at end step.
- A sector with your structure remains controlled by you while the structure survives.
- Adjacent influence from some worlds or structures can project control.
- If both sides project equal influence, the sector is contested.
- Center sectors grant extra Influence when controlled.

## Victory Conditions

### Conquest

Reduce the enemy commander to 0 HP.

This should be the easiest win condition to understand.

### Ascendancy

Reach 30 Influence.

This rewards board control, world development, and structures.

### Genesis

Complete a world-specific objective.

Examples:

- Charge three Astral Gates.
- Grow a Verdant Worldroot across five sectors.
- Keep a Solari Dawn Spire alive for three turns.
- Build three Synthari Relays in connected sectors.

### Campaign/Boss Objectives

Boss battles can override normal goals:

- Survive six anomaly pulses.
- Destroy the boss core.
- Protect evacuation sectors.
- Purify corrupted worlds.
- Stop a reactor meltdown.

## Readability Rules

The 5x5 board must never become visually confusing.

Required highlights:

- Legal deploy sectors
- Legal move sectors
- Legal attack targets
- Enemy threatened sectors
- Structure trigger radius
- World resource output
- Active hazards
- End-of-turn sector control preview
- Victory progress preview

Required tooltips:

- Keyword
- World
- Structure
- Commander ability
- Resource
- Status effect

## Alpha Board Prototype

The first 5x5 prototype should include:

- Player and enemy commander HP
- Resonance
- Hand of cards
- 25 animated sectors
- Deploy from hand
- Move 1 sector
- Attack adjacent
- One world card per faction
- One structure per faction
- One attachment type
- Damage popups
- Death animation
- Sector control coloring
- Influence counter
- Victory and defeat splash

No monetization. No full matchmaking. No complex deckbuilder rebuild.
